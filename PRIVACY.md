# Privacy

Christian's Download Any Video doesn't collect, store or send any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers: everything it does happens in your own browser.

When you click the button, it looks at the page in that tab to find the address of the video it is showing, and asks Chrome to download that address. The video goes straight to your downloads folder.

Nothing it sees leaves your computer, and none of it is shared with anyone.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/christianjbrown/chrome-extension-download-any-video/issues.
